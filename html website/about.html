About Mqadi Travels Car Hire

Reliable car rental solutions across KwaZulu-Natal and beyond

Who We Are

Mqadi Travels Car Hire is a proudly South African car rental agency based in Durban. We started with one goal: to provide affordable, clean, and reliable vehicles for business, tourism, and everyday travel without the stress of hidden fees.

Why Choose Mqadi Travels?

🚘 Wide Range of Vehicles

From economy cars and SUVs to 7-seater vans and luxury vehicles. Perfect for individuals, families, and groups.

💰 Affordable Rates

Transparent pricing with no hidden charges. Daily, weekly, and monthly rental options available.

📍 Convenient Pickup

We offer car delivery to King Shaka Airport, OR Tambo, your hotel, or home in Durban and surrounding areas.

✅ 24/7 Support

Breakdown assistance and customer support available around the clock for your peace of mind.

Our Mission

To make car hire simple, safe, and accessible for everyone. Whether you need a car for a weekend trip to Ballito, a business meeting in Pietermaritzburg, or a month-long rental, Mqadi Travels has the right vehicle for you.
